# Sofi Spare Parts — Website Overview & Requirements

## 1. Business Information

**Business Name:** Sofi Spare Parts
**Business Type:** Spare Parts & Automotive Products
**Location:** Shashamane 01, Ethiopia
**Opening:** 7 days a week
**Delivery:** Available

### Business Description

Sofi Spare Parts is a spare-parts and automotive products shop serving vehicle owners in Shashamane and surrounding areas. The business provides a wide range of quality spare parts, engine oils, filters, tires, wheels, batteries, and other essential automotive products.

The shop serves both **cars and Bajaj/TVS three-wheelers**, with separate sections for each type of vehicle to make it easier for customers to find the products they need.

The **Car Spare Parts** section focuses on products such as engine parts, engine oil, filters, batteries, tires, wheels, body parts, and other essential components for different vehicles, including Isuzu and other car models.

The **Bajaj/TVS Spare Parts** section provides motor and engine-related parts, pistons, engine oil, filters, and other essential components specifically for Bajaj and TVS three-wheelers.

Sofi Spare Parts also provides delivery services, making it easier for customers to receive the parts and products they need.

---

# 2. Website Goal

The main purpose of the website is to give customers a simple and professional way to:

* Learn about Sofi Spare Parts
* See what types of spare parts are available
* Choose between car and Bajaj/TVS spare parts
* View product and shop images
* Find the physical shop location
* Get directions through Google Maps
* Call the business directly
* Contact the business through WhatsApp and Telegram
* Visit the business's social media pages
* See customer reviews
* Learn about delivery availability
* Easily contact the shop from a mobile phone

The website should focus on **visibility, trust, easy communication, and finding the shop** rather than becoming a complicated online store.

# 18. Responsive Design

Mobile responsiveness is a priority.

The website should work properly on:

* Mobile phones
* Tablets
* Laptops
* Desktop monitors

The mobile version should not simply shrink the desktop version.

Instead, the layout should intelligently adapt.

Examples:

* Mobile navigation menu
* Stacked cards
* Full-width contact buttons
* Responsive Google Map
* Mobile-friendly gallery
* Large touch-friendly buttons
