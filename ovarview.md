1. 🧭 Navbar

Keep the navigation simple:

Logo / Shop Name
Home
Parts
About
Contact

Because this is a single-page website, clicking these should smoothly scroll to the corresponding section.

On mobile, use a hamburger menu.

2. 🚗 Hero Section

This is the first thing customers see.

Include:

Shop name

Quality Spare Parts for Your Vehicle

A short sentence explaining what the business provides.

Then two important buttons:

📞 Call Now

💬 WhatsApp

The phone and WhatsApp buttons should be highly visible because customers may come to the website specifically to contact the shop.

You can also have a professional image of spare parts or the shop in the hero section.

3. 🏪 About Us

Keep this short.

Explain:

What the business sells
What types of vehicles/parts you support
Where the shop is located
What makes the business useful to customers

Don't make this section too long.

4. 🔧 Spare Parts Categories

Show the main categories visually.

For example:

Engine Parts

Engine components, filters, belts, etc.

Brake Parts

Brake pads, discs, brake components, etc.

Electrical Parts

Batteries, bulbs, electrical components, etc.

Transmission

Transmission-related parts and components.

Body Parts

Mirrors, lights, body components, etc.

Tires & Wheels

Tires, wheels and related products.

Since this is only a homepage, you don't need a full product database. These can simply be category cards.

5. ⭐ Why Choose Us

This makes the business look more professional.

You could have 4–5 cards:

✓ Quality Parts

Reliable and suitable spare parts.

✓ Fair Prices

Competitive pricing for customers.

✓ Trusted Service

Helpful customer service.

✓ Convenient Location

Easy to find using Google Maps.

✓ Easy Communication

Call, WhatsApp or Telegram directly.

6. 📍 Find Us — Very Important

This should be one of the strongest sections.

Show:

Google Map

The map should point to the exact shop location.

Under the map:

📍 Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

or the actual shop address.

Then:

📍 Get Directions

When the customer clicks it, Google Maps opens.

This is especially useful for a physical spare-parts shop.

🎨 Design Direction

For the visual design, I recommend:

Style: Modern automotive / professional

Colors: Use the business's existing brand colors if it has them.

Typography: Clean and modern.

Cards: Slightly rounded with subtle shadows.

Buttons: Large enough to tap easily.

Animations: Small smooth animations when sections/cards appear.

Images: High-quality automotive/spare-parts images.

Icons: Lucide Icons or another consistent icon library.

Don't overdo animations. The website should feel like a real business website, not an animation demo.

Technology

For this project, keep it simple:

Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Lucide Icons + Google Maps + Vercel

You don't need a database or backend yet because this is a static business homepage.

⭐ The most important customer journey

Think about what a real customer does:

Customer opens website
        ↓
Sees what the shop sells
        ↓
Sees spare-parts categories
        ↓
Wants to visit
        ↓
Checks Google Map
        ↓
Clicks "Get Directions"
        ↓
OR
        ↓
Clicks Call / WhatsApp
        ↓
Contacts the shop

That should be the main purpose of the website.
